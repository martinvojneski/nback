    How to play
    1. Every few seconds a square lights up on the grid and a letter is spoken.
    2. Tap Position when the square is in the same spot as it was N turns ago.
    3. Tap Sound when the letter is the same as the one N turns ago.
    4. Both can match on the same turn — then tap both.

    2-back example — letters: K  R  K  T  K

    The 3rd letter matches the 1st, and the 5th matches the 3rd.

    Score 80% or more and you move up a level. Under 50% and you drop one. A few short sessions a day works best.

    No sound on iPhone? Turn up the volume, check the silent switch, or switch the sound to Tones in Settings.
